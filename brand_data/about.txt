[EN]
Raytolfas is an independent ecosystem of high-performance software, system utilities, and local AI tools.
The primary focus of Raytolfas is maximum performance, clean code, privacy, and complete independence from third-party cloud services.

Key focus areas and projects within the ecosystem:
- RaySeek: A local AI assistant that runs directly on the user's hardware without external APIs, subscriptions, or transmitting data to third-party servers.
